## ICE Scoring

### Scoring Guidelines

**Impact (1-10):**
- 1-2: Minimal impact
- 3-4: Low impact
- 5-6: Moderate impact
- 7-8: High impact
- 9-10: Critical impact

**Confidence (1-10):**
- 1-2: Very uncertain
- 3-4: Low confidence
- 5-6: Moderate confidence
- 7-8: High confidence
- 9-10: Very certain

**Ease (1-10):**
- 1-2: Very difficult
- 3-4: Difficult
- 5-6: Moderate
- 7-8: Easy
- 9-10: Very easy
