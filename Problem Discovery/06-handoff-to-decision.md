# 6. Handoff to Decision Tree

## Overview

The Problem Framing process outputs a pristine **Problem Statement** paired with one or more finalized **RFCs**. This package is now ready to enter the **Decision Tree** framework.

## The Connection Point

In the Decision Tree, Phase 1 is **State Gathering**. The very first dimension of State Gathering is the **Alignment Check**, which asks:

* *"Are both cofounders aligned on the problem statement?"*
* *"Do we share the same success metrics?"*

Because the Problem Framing process has already formalized the Problem Statement and Success Criteria, this alignment check should be nearly instantaneous.

## The Handoff Checklist

Before moving to the Decision Tree, ensure:

- [ ] The problem is in the Top 5 of the EB-25.
- [ ] The Problem Statement is documented and solution-agnostic.
- [ ] At least one RFC has been drafted, reviewed, and finalized.
- [ ] All major objections in the RFC have been documented as trade-offs or risks.

## Initiating the Decision

Take the Problem Statement and the RFC(s) and begin the **State Gathering Phase** in the Decision Tree. The RFCs will serve as the "Options" evaluated in whichever decision framework (ASOFF, Weighted Matrix, etc.) is ultimately selected.
